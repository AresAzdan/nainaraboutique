About Nainara

Nainara is a curated fashion & jewelry boutique based in Bogor, West Java. We believe in timeless elegance, ethically sourced materials, and craftsmanship that tells a story.

Every piece in our collection is carefully selected to help you express your personal style with confidence. From handcrafted jewelry to premium everyday essentials, we bring you the best of modern luxury.

Founded in 2026, Nainara started as a small passion project and has grown into a trusted name for quality fashion in Indonesia.

Privacy Policy

At Nainara, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide during registration, checkout, and account management, including your name, email, phone number, and shipping address.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, provide customer support, and improve our services. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal data. Payment processing is handled securely through Midtrans.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, reach us via WhatsApp at +62 811 1011 4443.

Terms of Service

By using the Nainara website and services, you agree to the following terms.

Orders & Payment

All orders must be paid within 24 hours. Unpaid orders will be automatically cancelled. Prices are in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) and include applicable taxes.

Shipping

We ship via trusted Indonesian couriers (JNE, J&T, SiCepat). Delivery times depend on your location and selected service. Tracking numbers are provided once your order is shipped.

Returns & Exchanges

Returns are accepted within 5 days of delivery for size exchanges only. Items must be unworn and in original condition. An unboxing video is required for all return requests.

Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Once shipped, cancellation is no longer available and the return policy applies.
